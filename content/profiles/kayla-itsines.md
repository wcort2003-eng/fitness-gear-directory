Kayla Itsines is an Australian personal trainer who has been training clients
since 2008 and built BBG (originally "Bikini Body Guide"), now delivered
through the **Sweat app**, into one of the most followed women's fitness
programs in the world.

## Training

From her published method, as reported by *Tom's Guide* and *Women's Health*:

- A structured **12-week program** built around short, high-intensity circuit
  sessions — **roughly 28 minutes each**, made up of **7-minute circuits** where
  you do as many rounds as possible.
- Typically around **three resistance sessions a week**, plus
  **low-intensity steady-state cardio (LISS)** and stretching/recovery days.
- Designed to be done **at home with minimal equipment**, which is a core part
  of the appeal.

## Diet

From her published approach, as reported by *Women's Health*:

- Promotes a **balanced, sustainable approach** to eating rather than extreme
  restriction, with a **meal plan included** in her program/app.
