Chris Hemsworth is best known for the "Thor" physique: large, lean, and
noticeably shoulder- and arm-dominant. It reads as attainable, but it is an
elite, job-funded result — see the reality check at the end before comparing
yourself to it.

## Training

As reported by *Men's Health Australia* and *Men's Fitness Australia*
(interviews with his trainer Luke Zocchi):

- **Four to five heavy weights sessions a week**, plus one lighter movement
  session.
- Sessions **rarely run over an hour**.
- A mix of traditional weightlifting and functional training, deliberately
  **emphasising shoulders and arms** to build the "Thor" look.
- **Boxing and HIIT** are worked in, sometimes training twice a day.
- Serious preparation starts **around three months before filming**.

## Diet

As reported by *Men's Fitness Australia* and *Men's Health Australia* (trainer
Luke Zocchi):

- Described as **clean and high in protein**.
- For bulking phases, the main lever is simply **eating much more than usual**.
- For one Thor preparation he followed a **strict vegan diet** — which cuts
  against the assumption that this physique needs heavy meat intake
  (reported by *Page Six*, via *Eat This, Not That!*).

## Supplements

No specific supplement stack is credibly documented beyond the obvious — a
high-protein diet, and protein through his own **Centr** brand. This profile
deliberately **does not list a stack it can't source.**

## Honest reality check

- He is **tall and genetically gifted** to begin with, works with a
  **full-time trainer and a private chef**, and is **paid to spend months**
  preparing.
- **What transfers** to a normal person: short, intense sessions of around an
  hour; a high-protein base; a shoulder-and-arm emphasis; and consistency.
- **What doesn't transfer**: the full-time prep setup and his genetic starting
  point.
- No credible source makes any claim about performance-enhancing drugs either
  way, so this profile **stays silent on that** rather than speculating.
